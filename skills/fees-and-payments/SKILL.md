# Fees and payments

## Quick answers
- Course prices range from **$200 to $500 USD**.
- All prices **include the curriculum and certification** — no hidden fees.
- 4 courses are currently **On Special** (discounted, starting Oct 19, 2026).
- 5 "Coming Soon" courses have no price announced yet — contact admin@aicacademy.ai.

## Details
- Exact prices per course are in `fees.yaml`. Always read that file before quoting a price; never guess.
- "On Special" means the course is available at a promotional price.
- Courses with no price listed are "Coming Soon" — the price will be announced when enrolment opens.

## When to hand off
- Payment problems, invoices, or refund requests: take a message for admin@aicacademy.ai.
- Corporate training pricing (bulk enrolment): take a message for admin@aicacademy.ai.
- Refund policy details: not published on the website as of 2026-10-13 — take a message.

# Programs and courses

## Quick answers
- AICA offers **18 courses** across 3 tracks: AI (13), Cyber Security (1), GRC & Data Protection (4).
- **4 courses start October 19, 2026** (On Special): CEH ($500), CCT ($400), AI Essentials ($200), CASP ($400).

# Programs: frequently asked questions

## What courses do you offer? / តើសាលាមានវគ្គសិក្សាអ្វីខ្លះ?
AICA offers 18 courses across three tracks: Artificial Intelligence (13 courses), Cyber Security (1 course), and GRC & Data Protection (4 courses). Read the full list in `programs.yaml`.

## What are the three tracks? / តើមានផ្នែកអ្វីខ្លះ?
1. **Artificial Intelligence** — from AI literacy to agentic AI and prompt engineering.
2. **Cyber Security** — ethical hacking (CEH), cybersecurity technician (CCT), cyber resilience for leaders.
3. **GRC & Data Protection** — Cambodia's LPDP law, DPO certification, DPIA workshops.

## Which courses are available now? / តើវគ្គណាខ្លះអាចចុះឈ្មោះបាន?
Four courses start **October 19, 2026** (On Special):
- Certified Ethical Hacker (C|EH) v13 — $500, 40 hours
- Certified Cybersecurity Technician (C|CT) — $400, 40 hours
- Artificial Intelligence Essentials — $200, 16 hours
- Certified AI Solution Practitioner (CASP) — $400, 40 hours

The other 14 courses are **Coming Soon** — dates to be announced.

## How long does each course take? / តើរៀនប៉ុន្មានថ្ងៃ?
- 40 hours (about 5 days): CEH, CCT, CASP
- 16 hours (about 2 days): AI Essentials
- 4 days: Certified DPO for Cambodia
- 3 days: Practitioner Certificate LPDP
- 2 days: most AI foundation and advanced courses
- 1 day: LPDP Awareness, DPIA Workshop, CyberResilience for Leaders

## How much do courses cost? / តើថ្លៃប៉ុន្មាន?
Prices range from $200 to $500 USD. All prices include the curriculum and certification. See `programs.yaml` or the fees-and-payments skill for the full price table.

## Do I need coding experience? / តើត្រូវចេះសរសេរកូដមុនចូលរៀនទេ?
It depends on the course. "Vibe Code with AI — Foundation" explicitly says **no prior coding experience required**. Most AI foundation courses do not list prerequisites. Technical courses like CASP involve Python. Contact admin@aicacademy.ai if you are unsure.

## Are classes taught in English or Khmer? / តើបង្រៀនជាភាសាអង់គ្លេស ឬខ្មែរ?
Not published on the website as of 2026-10-13. Contact admin@aicacademy.ai for details.

## What certifications can I earn? / តើខ្ញុំអាចទទួលវិញ្ញាបនបត្រអ្វីខ្លះ?
- **EC-Council**: Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH), Certified Cybersecurity Technician (CCT)
- **AICA**: All other courses issue an AICA certification upon completion.
Each course price includes the certification.

## Is there a pathway for data protection courses? / តើមានផ្លូវរៀនការការពារទិន្នន័យទេ?
Yes — the recommended order is:
1. LPDP Awareness for All Employees (1 day)
2. Practitioner Certificate for LPDP 2025 (3 days)
3. Certified DPO for Cambodia (4 days, capstone)
4. DPIA Workshop (1 day, specialist)

## Can I study part-time or on weekends? / តើអាចរៀនចុងសប្ដាហ៍បានទេ?
Not published on the website as of 2026-10-13. Contact admin@aicacademy.ai to ask about scheduling options.

## What tools will I use in the AI courses? / តើវគ្គ AI ប្រើឧបករណ៍អ្វីខ្លះ?
Depends on the course. CASP uses Python, Roboflow, YOLO, OpenAI models, Streamlit, and Agentic AI frameworks. Prompt Engineering courses use ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Vibe Code uses Claude and Cursor.

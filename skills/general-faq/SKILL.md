# General FAQ

## Quick answers
- AICA stands for **AI & Cybersecurity Academy**.
- Backed by **DACTA Global Academy (Singapore)** — same curriculum, quality assurance and standards.
- Legal entity: **VANGUARD AI & DIGITAL ACADEMY CO., LTD.**
- Three tracks: **Artificial Intelligence**, **Cyber Security**, **GRC & Data Protection**.
- Programmes follow the **Singapore Skills Framework** benchmark.
- GRC courses align with Cambodia's **Law on Personal Data Protection (LPDP)**, effective January 2026.
- Trainers hold certifications from EC-Council, CompTIA, ISC2, Cisco, Oracle, Microsoft, and Google.
- Certificate verification: https://www.aicacademy.ai/en/certificates

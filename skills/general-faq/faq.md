# General: frequently asked questions

## What is AICA? / តើ AICA ជាអ្វី?
AICA (AI & Cybersecurity Academy) is a Singapore-backed professional training academy in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. It offers certified courses in Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, and GRC & Data Protection for professionals, enterprises, and government organisations.

## Who backs AICA? / តើអ្នកណាគាំទ្រ AICA?
AICA is built on DACTA Global Academy's regional expertise, partnerships, and standards. DACTA operates in Singapore, Vietnam, and the Philippines. AICA uses the same curriculum, quality assurance, and instructional design as DACTA.

## What is DACTA? / តើ DACTA ជាអ្វី?
DACTA Global Academy is an international training institution based in Singapore. AICA is their Cambodia academy, bringing Singapore-standard AI and cybersecurity training to the local market.

## What is the mission of AICA? / តើបេសកកម្មរបស់ AICA ជាអ្វី?
To empower enterprises and build competent workforces across Southeast Asia by developing professionals in AI and cybersecurity — human-focused, internationally standardised, and adapted to local markets.

## What is the vision? / តើចក្ខុវិស័យជាអ្វី?
To become the leading international academy for AI and cybersecurity professional development in ASEAN, anchored in Singapore standards.

## What standards do AICA programmes follow? / តើកម្មវិធីសិក្សាធ្វើតាមស្តង់ដារអ្វី?
Programmes are built to the Singapore Skills Framework benchmark. GRC courses are aligned with Cambodia's Law on Personal Data Protection (LPDP 2025) and the ASEAN Guide on AI Governance & Ethics (2024).

## Is AICA accredited? / តើ AICA ត្រូវបានទទួលស្គាល់ទេ?
AICA's programmes follow Singapore Skills Framework standards via the DACTA partnership. Specific government accreditation details are not published on the website — contact admin@aicacademy.ai for the latest accreditation status.

## What certifications can I get? / តើខ្ញុំអាចទទួលវិញ្ញាបនបត្រអ្វីខ្លះ?
Courses prepare you for industry certifications from bodies like EC-Council (CEH, CCT), CompTIA, ISC2, Cisco, Oracle, Microsoft, and Google. Each course includes its own AICA certification upon completion.

## How do I verify a certificate? / តើខ្ញុំត្រូវផ្ទៀងផ្ទាត់វិញ្ញាបនបត្រយ៉ាងម៉េច?
Visit the certificate verification directory at https://www.aicacademy.ai/en/certificates to search and validate official certificates issued by AICA.

## Who are the trainers? / តើអ្នកណាជាគ្រូបង្រៀន?
AICA has 6 trainers (4 Lead Instructors, 2 Instructors). They include government cybersecurity specialists, PhD holders in IT, and professionals with certifications from EC-Council, CompTIA, ISC2, Cisco, Oracle, Microsoft, and Google. See the full list at https://www.aicacademy.ai/en/teachers.
